Ready to Storm?

VISION ▼
To be a center of excellence in Mechanical Engineering education and research, nurturing innovative engineers to develop sustainable and socially impactful technological solutions.
MISSION ▼
  1. To provide transformative, experiential mechanical engineering education that cultivates critical thinking, technical competence, innovation, and professional ethics.
  2. To promote interdisciplinary research and product development focused on sustainable, energy-efficient, and human-centric engineering solutions.
  3. To bridge the gap between engineering education and societal needs through technology transfer, industry collaboration, and community-driven initiatives.

About Association

STORM is the official student association of the Department of Mechanical Engineering at the College of Engineering Pathanapuram. We strive to bridge the gap between academic curriculum and industry requirements.

Our goal is to foster a community of innovative thinkers, ethical professionals, and technically sound engineers ready to tackle modern industrial challenges.
